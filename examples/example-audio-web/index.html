Classify sound live

Runs an edge-ml model that was trained on sound (preset audio_cnn or mixed_cnn) on this device's microphone, in the page, with onnxruntime-web. Sound is resampled from the microphone's rate to the model's. Models that also take sensors use the phone's motion sensor.

Model from the edge-ml server
or from a download (ONNX): model.onnx and manifest.json
